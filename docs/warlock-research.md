# Warlock research: WoW Forever, 1-42 (2026-11-07)

[V] means two or more sources agree, or QuestieDB confirms it. [S] means only one source says it. [Q] means it was checked in Forever QuestieDB. The numbers in brackets are the sources listed at the end of this file. Most of these guides were written during the beta (capped at level 20/30), so treat their advice past level 30 as untested.

## Forever changes that matter
- **Talent trees are new.** Improved Drain Soul, Improved Curse of Weakness, Malediction and Dark Pact are gone [6]. Each talent row needs 5 more points spent in its tree [7]. You get one point per level from level 10 [5]. The "Talented" Legacy perk gives points up to 5 levels earlier [1][5]. [V]
- **Banes and Curses are separate.** Banes are damage over time; Curses are utility. You can have one of each on a target. Bane of Agony replaces Curse of Agony and tags the mob instantly [1][3][4]. [V]
- **DoTs can crit**, so Pandemic and the Human racial Ruthless matter [1][2][3][4]. [V]
- **Spell scaling:** Life Tap scales with Spirit and pets scale with your gear [1][3][4]. [V]
- **Drain Soul** can give a shard on any tick [1][4]. [V]
- **Fear** has a 20-yard range [1][4]. [V]
- **Firestone and Spellstone** are now weapon imbues [3][4]. [V]
- **Wands** no longer scale with spell power, and you must stand still for 0.5 s before shooting [4]. [S]
- **Dungeon XP is lower:** dungeon quests give half the bonus XP they did before, and dungeon mobs give less XP [4]. [S]
- **Riding and dual spec** both unlock at level 40 [1][5]. [V]
- **No debuff limit** [3]. Curse of Shadow is removed; Curse of the Elements now covers all magic schools [3]. [S]

## Rotation by level band
- **1-10:** Shadow Bolt, then Immolate. Add Corruption from level 4 and Bane of Agony from level 8. Use a wand, then Life Tap. Fear a second mob. [V]
- **10-20:** Pull with Bane of Agony and Corruption, add Immolate on mobs that live long enough, then finish with your wand or Drain Life. The Voidwalker tanks, so you can take two mobs at once. [V]/[S]
- **20-30:** Same opener. The Sayaad (Succubus/Incubus) kills faster; "drain tanking" (tank the mob yourself and keep healing with Drain Life) works from about level 20 [1][2][3].
- **30-42:** Drain Life becomes your filler. Life Tap whenever your health is full. Use the wand only to finish a mob [1]. [S]

## Training
The trainer lines in the guide follow Bethink's Forever lists, and [5] confirms them level by level:
- Demon Armor 20, Hellfire 30, Spellstone 36, Howl of Terror 40, Death Coil 42. [V]
- Shadowburn and Conflagrate are now talents.

No source gives Forever training costs or grimoire prices; these are unknown.

## Professions
- **Tailoring Shadow/Pristine sets:** skill 60 to 125, bought with Merchant's Favor [9][10]. The Filigreed patterns drop in dungeons.
- **Soul Pouch:** skill 215 on Forever [9] (260 in Classic). This is a conflict between sources.
- **Felcloth Bag:** skill 285 [9]. [S]
- **Enchanting:** Lesser Magic Wand (character level 5), Greater Magic Wand (character level 13), Soulstaff (skill 140, +34 spell power), Twisted Nether Wand (skill 185). The skill requirements for the wands conflict between sources (70 vs 110) [12][13].
