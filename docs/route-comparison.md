# Route comparison (short version)

This is how the 1-42 route was checked against RestedXP's Forever routes and against web guides. Only the ideas were used: RestedXP is not GPL, so none of its text is copied here or in the guide.

## What the comparison found (it led to 0.3.0 and 0.4.0)
1. **Darkshore and Ashenvale at 16-21.** Every RestedXP variant uses these zones. They replaced a stretched Westfall at 14-19 and early Redridge group quests at 19-22.
2. **Fewer trips between hubs.** Westfall, Lakeshire and Stormwind were visited back and forth too often. The 23-25 transit loop and the 34-35 trip to Desolace were cut down.
3. **Group quests at the right level.** Redridge's elite quests were moved to 25-28. Group quests are `[O]` or marked "form a party".
4. **Class travel used as route steps.** Mage teleports are now steps in the route.
5. **Quests added for Forever** that sit on the route at no extra travel.

## What the route does better than RestedXP
- It covers more dungeons, with their quests collected beforehand and finished in one visit: Hall of Thanes, Deadmines, SFK, BFD, Stockades, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, Gnomeregan, RFK, SM, Drowned City and Razorfen Downs.
- It includes Forever's new hubs and quest chains.
- It is one continuous chain from 1 to 42, with level gates, safety notes and training stops in cities. RestedXP's free Forever routes stop at 21.

## Caveats
- The time savings were estimated, not measured.
- Forever's QuestieDB has no quest XP values. The XP budget uses figures from the web and comparisons with similar quests.
- Check Forever flight paths and boat routes before you change the order of the route.
