# Level XP reference

XP to next level is the full bar required at the current level. Total XP is the cumulative amount required to reach that level. Verify the curve when adapting to a different game version.

## Planning heuristics

- Ordinary outdoor and delivery quests: estimate **2 × nominal reward XP in total**. This includes objective combat, exploration and variable bonuses; do not add them again.
- Dungeon quests and direct boss-drop quests: count reward XP **once**. Add **0.2 × the entry level's XP bar** for the dungeon's single clear. Use the planned entry level and update it when the plan changes.
- Treat these coefficients as earnings estimates. Allow for level-dependent reward reductions and avoid stacking additional patch or bonus multipliers.
- Count rewards at turn-in. Carry surplus XP across chapters and calculate checkpoints chronologically; later rewards cannot justify earlier encounters.
- Keep unknown rewards unpriced. Check the effect of unavailable shares and personal chance starters; sharing does not bypass prerequisites.
- Use roughly 10% of a level as a review threshold for checkpoint shortfalls, weighing catch-up against travel savings.

Examples: 1,000 nominal outdoor reward XP estimates 2,000 total XP. A clear entered at level 18 adds 0.2 × 19,400 = 3,880 combat XP, plus its quest rewards once.
